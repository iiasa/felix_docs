# FeliX-Lifestyle

FeliX-Lifestyle extends FeliX with sectorial energy-demand and lifestyle-change modules. The documentation includes the representation of sectorial energy demand and the endogenous lifestyle module linking socioeconomic context, cognition, behaviour, and demand-side outcomes.
